An Icosahedral Wing Geometry Framework for Small Vertical-Axis Wind Turbines

Golden-ratio-based blade layout and torque balance analysis for GeoWind vertical-axis wind turbine systems.
Download PDF View on Zenodo

Abstract

This paper proposes a novel blade arrangement framework for small vertical-axis wind turbines (VAWTs) derived from the geometric properties of the regular icosahedron.

Building upon a previously established ten-face non-edge-sharing wing configuration based on golden-ratio geometry, the study formalizes a reproducible aerodynamic layout suitable for CAD implementation and structural interpretation.

The work demonstrates that the pole-opposite edge midpoints form a regular decagon on the equatorial plane with radius:

R = (phi / 2) * l

where phi denotes the golden ratio and l represents the edge length of the regular icosahedron.

The framework is proposed as a geometric basis for future hybrid lift-drag VAWT systems and subsequent tetrahedral self-aligned blade extensions.
